# Primitive: associate(seed, distance)

**What:** Starting from a seed concept, retrieve related concepts. Push toward remote associations for novelty.

**Why:** Mednick's associative theory — novel ideas come from combining associatively remote elements. Close associations give obvious ideas. Far associations give surprising ones.

## Distance Modes

| Distance | What | Use For |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Near** | Things clearly related to the seed | Completeness, low novelty |
| **Medium** | Analogies within the same broad domain | Adjacent innovation |
| **Far** | Cross-domain analogies and forced connections (bisociation) | Breakthrough innovation |
| **Random** | Genuinely random stimulus, force a connection | Breaking fixation |

## Random Entry Protocol

1. State the random stimulus (word, object, image concept)
2. List 3-5 attributes or associations of that stimulus
3. For each attribute, force a connection to the problem
4. At least 1 will feel absurd — push through it, absurdity often produces novelty

## Output

List of associations with distance tags. Far and random associations marked as fragile — nurture them, don't judge feasibility yet.
