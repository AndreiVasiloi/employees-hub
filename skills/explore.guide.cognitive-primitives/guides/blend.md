# Primitive: blend(domainA, domainB)

**What:** Construct a conceptual blend — take partial structure from two domains and create an emergent hybrid with properties neither input has alone.

**Why:** Fauconnier and Turner's blending theory. The blend is not just "A + B" — it produces emergent structure.

## Protocol

1. **Identify input spaces:** Two domains/concepts being blended
2. **Map counterparts:** What elements in A correspond to elements in B?
3. **Find the generic space:** What abstract structure do they share?
4. **Construct the blend:** Combine selected elements, then ask:
   - What new properties emerge from the combination?
   - What inferences can you draw in the blend that aren't available in either input?
5. **Run the blend:** Elaborate the blended concept — follow its internal logic

## Example

```
Domain A: Subscription box (curated, surprise, recurring, delight)
Domain B: Medical diagnosis (personalized, data-driven, adaptive, progressive)

Generic: "Personalized delivery of something tailored to your state"

Blend: "A product recommendation system that diagnoses your current
situation/mood/need and prescribes curated micro-experiences, adapting
the prescription as it learns your response patterns"

Emergent: The idea of "prescribing" experiences adds authority and trust
framing that neither subscription nor diagnosis has alone.
```

**Template:** `templates/blend-worksheet.md`

## Output

Blend description + emergent properties + 2-3 concrete ideas from running the blend's logic.
