# Step 7: Identify Open Questions and Risks

## Objective

Document what needs resolution, prioritized by impact on wireframe completion.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 6 (Cross-Screen Patterns) complete with human validation

## Actions

### 7.1 Categorize Open Questions

**High priority** (blocks wireframe completion):
- Question, why it matters, decision owner, how to resolve

**Medium priority** (can proceed with assumptions):
- Question, why it matters, assumption for now, validation plan

### 7.2 Document Risks

- Risks related to IA not being confirmed
- Risks related to data constraints
- Any other wireframe-specific risks

**STOP**: Wait for human to address high-priority questions.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Open questions prioritized
- [ ] Risks documented
- [ ] Human has addressed high-priority questions
