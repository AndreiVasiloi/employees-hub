# Step 5: Define Labeling and Taxonomy

## Objective

Create controlled vocabulary with section labels, screen labels, preferred terms, synonyms, and facets.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 4 (Sitemap) complete with human validation

## Actions

### 5.1 Define Labels and Vocabulary

```
Labeling and Taxonomy

**Section labels** (main navigation):
- [Label 1]: [Purpose]
- [Label 2]: [Purpose]
- [Label 3]: [Purpose]

**Screen labels** (page titles):
- [Label]: [Purpose]
- [Label]: [Purpose]

**Controlled vocabulary**:

Preferred terms:
- [Term]: [Definition]
- [Term]: [Definition]

Synonyms (acceptable in content, not in navigation):
- [Synonym] for [Preferred term]

Do not use:
- [Term]: [Reason - e.g., "too technical", "ambiguous"]

**Facets and filters** (if applicable):
- Facet: [Name]
  - Values: [Examples]
  - Default: [Default sort/filter]
  - Where used: [Screens]

Are these labels clear and consistent? Any terms that need adjustment?
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to validate labeling.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Section and screen labels defined
- [ ] Controlled vocabulary established with preferred terms
- [ ] Synonyms documented (acceptable vs prohibited)
- [ ] Facets and filters defined where applicable
- [ ] Human has validated labeling
