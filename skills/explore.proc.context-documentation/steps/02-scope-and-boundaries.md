# Step 2: Scope & Boundaries

## Objective

Define what's in and out of scope with detailed categorization and boundary conditions.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 1 (Problem Statement) complete with confirmed problem analysis

## Actions

### 2.1 Define Scope

Document scope with detailed categorization:

```
Scope & Boundaries

### In Scope

Core Platform:
- ✅ [Core capability 1 with technology/approach]
- ✅ [Core capability 2 with technology/approach]

Pages & Features (if applicable):
- ✅ [Feature 1]
- ✅ [Feature 2]

Infrastructure:
- ✅ [Infrastructure component 1]
- ✅ [Infrastructure component 2]

### Out of Scope

Explicitly excluded:
- ❌ [Excluded item 1] — TBD in SP2 (needs stakeholder clarification)
- ❌ [Excluded item 2] — [Reason for exclusion]

### Boundary Conditions

Integration boundaries:
- Platform consumes [external system] (read/write for [purpose])
- Platform writes to [external system] ([purpose])

Ownership boundaries:
- [Organization] builds: [Components owned]
- [External party] provides: [Components provided]
- [Infrastructure]: Ownership TBD in SP2 (needs clarification)

Confirm scope or tell me what to add/remove.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to validate scope.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] In-scope items categorized (Core Platform, Features, Infrastructure)
- [ ] Out-of-scope items explicitly documented with reasons
- [ ] Integration boundaries defined
- [ ] Ownership boundaries defined
- [ ] Human validated scope
