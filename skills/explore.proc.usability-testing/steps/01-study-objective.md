# Step 1: Define Study Objective and Scope

## Objective

Clarify what you're testing and why, including scope, decisions to inform, success definition, and key risks.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Wireframes or prototype available
- [ ] User flows and personas available
- [ ] Slug confirmed

## Actions

### 1.1 Define Study Objective

What is this test meant to validate or learn?

### 1.2 Define What is Being Tested

- Experience scope (feature/product area)
- Artifacts (wireframes, low-fi prototype, hi-fi prototype, live product)
- Flows and screens in scope

### 1.3 Primary Decisions This Study Will Inform

List the specific design decisions this study will resolve.

### 1.4 Success Definition

- Task success rate target (e.g., >80%)
- Time on task target (if relevant)
- Critical issues threshold (what would block release)

### 1.5 Key Risks Being Tested

- Findability risk
- Comprehension risk
- Workflow risk
- Trust or safety risk

**STOP**: Wait for human to confirm objective and scope.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Study objective defined
- [ ] Scope and artifacts specified
- [ ] Decisions to inform listed
- [ ] Success definition established
- [ ] Human has confirmed objective and scope
