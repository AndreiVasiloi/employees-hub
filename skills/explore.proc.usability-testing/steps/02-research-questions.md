# Step 2: Define Research Questions and Hypotheses

## Objective

Document what you need to learn, with optional hypotheses and supporting/disproving evidence.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 1 (Study Objective) complete with human confirmation

## Actions

### 2.1 Define Research Questions

Based on the objective, create specific, answerable questions:
- RQ1: Can users find [action] from [starting point]?
- RQ2: Do users understand [concept/label] without explanation?
- RQ3: Can users recover from [error/edge case] without assistance?

### 2.2 Define Hypotheses (Optional but Recommended)

For each hypothesis:
- The hypothesis statement
- Evidence that supports it (from IA/wireframes)
- Evidence that disproves it (what would indicate it's wrong)

**STOP**: Wait for human to validate research questions.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Research questions defined (3-5 questions)
- [ ] Hypotheses documented with evidence criteria
- [ ] Human has validated research questions
