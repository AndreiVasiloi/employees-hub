# Step 10: Write Usability Test Plan

## Objective

Write the complete usability test plan to `explore/design/usability-test-plan-[slug].md`.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 9 (Analysis Plan) complete with human confirmation

## Actions

### 10.1 Write Document

Write complete test plan to `explore/design/usability-test-plan-[slug].md`.

**Use template**: `templates/usability-test-plan-template.md`

**Required sections**:
1. Snapshot (objective, what is being tested, decisions, success definition, risks)
2. Research Questions and Hypotheses
3. Method (study type, format, stimulus)
4. Participants (profile, screening, sample size, recruitment)
5. Scenarios and Tasks (task structure, tasks with success criteria)
6. Metrics and Data Capture (behavioral, attitudinal, qualitative)
7. Moderation Guide (introduction, warm-up, task prompts, wrap-up)
8. Logistics (tools, roles, session checklist)
9. Analysis Plan (synthesis, severity rating, deliverables)
10. Risks and Mitigations
11. Approvals and Sign-off
12. Acceptance Criteria

### 10.2 Verify Document

Confirm all 12 sections present with no placeholder text remaining.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Document written to `explore/design/usability-test-plan-[slug].md`
- [ ] All 12 sections present and populated
- [ ] No placeholder text remaining
