# Step 2: Gather Information Per Persona

## Objective

For each persona, gather information from the human about demographics, goals, pain points, behaviors, mental models, devices, and values.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 1 (Identify Personas) complete with types confirmed

## Actions

### 2.1 Request Information for Each Persona

For each persona, ask:

```
Persona [N]: [Type]

I need information to build this persona. Please provide what you know:

1. Demographics (age, location, occupation, education, technical proficiency)
2. Goals & Motivations (what they want to achieve)
3. Pain Points (current challenges they face)
4. Behaviors (how they act in relevant contexts)
5. Mental Models (how they think about the problem)
6. Devices & Channels (primary/secondary devices, usage context)
7. Values (what matters to them beyond the task)

Provide as much detail as you have. I'll tag items as [VALIDATED] if you confirm them,
or [ASSUMPTION] if I need to infer them.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human input for each persona.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Information gathered for all personas
- [ ] Human has provided what they know for each persona
