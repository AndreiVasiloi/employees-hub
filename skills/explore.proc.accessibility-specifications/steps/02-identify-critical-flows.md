# Step 2: Identify Critical User Flows and Screens

## Objective

Select P0 flows and high-risk screens that require accessibility specifications.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 1 (Establish Baseline) complete with confirmed baseline
- [ ] Wireframes available for review

## Actions

### 2.1 Identify P0 Flows, High-Risk Screens, and Components

Based on wireframes, identify critical areas:

```
Critical Flows and Screens for Accessibility

Based on the wireframes, I've identified these critical areas:

**P0 flows** (must be fully accessible):
- [Flow 1 - e.g., "Sign up and onboarding"]
- [Flow 2 - e.g., "Create and submit form"]
- [Flow 3 - e.g., "Search and filter results"]

**High-risk screens** (complex interactions or critical tasks):
- [Screen 1 - e.g., "Multi-step form with validation"]
- [Screen 2 - e.g., "Data table with sorting and filtering"]
- [Screen 3 - e.g., "Dashboard with dynamic updates"]

**High-risk components**:
- [Component 1 - e.g., "Custom date picker"]
- [Component 2 - e.g., "File upload with drag-and-drop"]
- [Component 3 - e.g., "Modal dialogs"]

Are these the right priorities for accessibility specifications?
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to validate priorities.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] P0 flows identified and confirmed
- [ ] High-risk screens listed
- [ ] High-risk components identified
- [ ] Human validated priorities
