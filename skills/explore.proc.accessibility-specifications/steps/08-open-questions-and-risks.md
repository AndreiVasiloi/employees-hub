# Step 8: Identify Open Questions and Risks

## Objective

Document unresolved questions, accessibility risks, and known limitations that need resolution.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 7 (Test Plan and Acceptance) complete with confirmed criteria
- [ ] All requirements documented from previous steps

## Actions

### 8.1 Document Questions and Risks

Identify what needs resolution:

```
Open Questions and Risks

**High priority questions**:
1. [Question - e.g., "What screen reader should be primary for testing?"]
   - Why it matters: [Impact]
   - How to resolve: [Method]

**Risks**:
- [Risk 1 - e.g., "Custom date picker may not meet WCAG 2.2 requirements"]
  - Mitigation: [Strategy]
- [Risk 2 - e.g., "Third-party component library has known accessibility issues"]
  - Mitigation: [Strategy]

**Known limitations**:
- [Limitation 1 - with rationale and workaround]

What questions can you help resolve now?
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to address high-priority questions.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] High-priority questions documented with resolution methods
- [ ] Accessibility risks identified with mitigation strategies
- [ ] Known limitations documented with workarounds
- [ ] Human addressed resolvable questions
