# Step 4: Identify Market Gaps

## Objective

Analyze what's missing in the current market and what our product uniquely provides.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 3 (Competitive Landscape) complete

## Actions

### 4.1 Document Current Solutions and Gaps

```
Market Gap Analysis

**What exists today**:
- [Solution type 1] — Provides X but lacks Y
- [Solution type 2] — Provides Y but lacks Z

**What our product provides** (market gap):
- [Feature 1] — No existing solution offers this
- [Feature 2] — Existing solutions offer this but poorly
- [Feature 3] — Combination that doesn't exist today
```

### 4.2 Validate Gaps

- Is this gap real or perceived?
- Why hasn't anyone filled this gap yet?
- What evidence supports this gap?

### 4.3 Define Competitive Advantage

- What makes our approach unique?
- What barriers to entry exist?

Document gaps with evidence from competitive analysis.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Current solution types documented with their limitations
- [ ] Market gaps identified with evidence
- [ ] Competitive advantage articulated
- [ ] Gap validity assessed
