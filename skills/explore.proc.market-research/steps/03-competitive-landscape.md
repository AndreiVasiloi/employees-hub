# Step 3: Analyze Competitive Landscape

## Objective

Map existing players and their offerings across direct, indirect, and adjacent categories.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 2 (Market Size) complete

## Actions

### 3.1 Identify Competitors

Identify and analyze competitors in these categories:

1. **Direct competitors**: Companies offering similar solutions
2. **Indirect competitors**: Alternative solutions to the same problem
3. **Adjacent players**: Companies that could enter the market

### 3.2 Analyze Each Competitor

For each competitor, document:
- Company name and description
- Offering (what they provide)
- Strengths (what they do well)
- Gaps (what they don't provide — opportunity for us)
- Market position (leader, challenger, niche)

### 3.3 Sources to Check

- Company websites and product pages
- G2, Capterra, TrustRadius reviews
- Crunchbase and funding databases
- Industry analyst reports
- LinkedIn company pages

Use `search_web` and `read_url_content` tools. Create comparison tables showing feature gaps.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Direct competitors identified and analyzed
- [ ] Indirect competitors identified and analyzed
- [ ] Adjacent players identified
- [ ] Comparison tables created showing gaps
- [ ] All competitor data sourced
