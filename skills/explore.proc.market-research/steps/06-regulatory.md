# Step 6: Research Regulatory and Compliance Context

## Objective

Identify industry standards, compliance requirements, and certifications needed.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 5 (Supply and Demand) complete

## Actions

### 6.1 Research Industry Standards

```
Industry standards:
- What standards apply to this industry?
- What certifications are required?
- What best practices exist?
```

### 6.2 Research Compliance Requirements

```
Compliance requirements:
- What regulations apply? (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, etc.)
- What data security requirements exist?
- What industry-specific regulations apply?
```

### 6.3 Define Compliance Strategy

```
Compliance strategy:
- MVP requirements: [What's needed for launch?]
- Phase 2 requirements: [What can be added later?]
```

### 6.4 Sources to Check

- Industry association websites
- Government regulatory bodies
- Compliance certification providers
- Competitor compliance pages

Document compliance requirements with sources.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Industry standards documented
- [ ] Compliance requirements identified with sources
- [ ] Compliance strategy defined (MVP vs later phases)
