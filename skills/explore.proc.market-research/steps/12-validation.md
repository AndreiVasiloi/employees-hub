# Step 12: Validation

## Objective

Run a completeness check on the market research document.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 11 (Write Document) complete
- [ ] Document exists at `explore/explore-[slug]/market-research.md`

## Actions

### 12.1 Run Completeness Check

Verify all required elements:

- [ ] Market size and growth data documented with sources
- [ ] Competitive landscape analyzed (direct, indirect, adjacent)
- [ ] Market gaps identified with evidence
- [ ] Supply and demand validated (if applicable)
- [ ] Regulatory requirements documented
- [ ] Technology trends researched
- [ ] MVP market sized with assumptions
- [ ] Competitive positioning defined
- [ ] Assumptions tagged as VALIDATED or NEEDS VALIDATION
- [ ] Sources cited for all claims

### 12.2 Present Validation Results

```
Market Research Complete

File: explore/explore-[slug]/market-research.md
Market size: [Value and CAGR]
Competitors analyzed: [N] companies
Market gaps identified: [N] gaps
Assumptions validated: [N] validated, [N] need further validation

Ready for user research to validate demand assumptions.
```

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All completeness checks pass
- [ ] Validation results presented to human
- [ ] Document ready for downstream consumption
