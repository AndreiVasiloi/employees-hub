# Step 7: Identify Technology Trends

## Objective

Research emerging technologies and innovations relevant to the product domain.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 6 (Regulatory) complete

## Actions

### 7.1 Research Emerging Technologies

For each relevant technology:

```
**[Technology name]**
- Status: [Emerging / Pilot / Scaling / Mature / Declining]
- Benefits: [What it enables]
- Examples: [Companies using it]
- Opportunity: [How we could use it]
```

### 7.2 Identify Industry Innovations

- What new approaches are being tested?
- What technologies are gaining traction?
- What's on the horizon (3-5 years)?

### 7.3 Define Technology Strategy

```
Technology strategy:
- MVP approach: [What technology to use now]
- Phase 2 approach: [What to integrate later]
- Phase 3+ approach: [Future opportunities]
```

Document trends with examples and timelines.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Emerging technologies researched with status
- [ ] Industry innovations documented
- [ ] Technology strategy defined per phase
