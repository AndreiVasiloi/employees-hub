# Step 2: Research Market Size and Growth

## Objective

Gather quantitative data on market opportunity including current size, projections, CAGR, drivers, and segments.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 1 (Research Scope) complete with human confirmation

## Actions

### 2.1 Gather Market Data

Research and document:

**Global market data**:
- Current market size (latest year available)
- Projected market size (5-10 year forecast)
- CAGR (compound annual growth rate)
- Largest geographic regions
- Market segments and their sizes

**Market drivers**:
- What factors are driving growth?
- What trends are accelerating demand?
- What external factors impact the market?

**Market segments**:
- How is the market segmented? (by application, mode, end-use, etc.)
- Which segments are largest?
- Which segments are growing fastest?

### 2.2 Sources to Check

- Industry research reports (Grand View Research, Gartner, Forrester, IDC)
- Government statistics and trade associations
- Public company financial reports
- Academic research and white papers

Use `search_web` and `read_url_content` tools to gather data. Document findings with sources and dates.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Market size data documented with sources
- [ ] Growth projections and CAGR documented
- [ ] Market drivers identified
- [ ] Market segments analyzed
- [ ] All claims cited with sources
