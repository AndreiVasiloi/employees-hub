# Step 5: Analyze Supply and Demand

## Objective

Validate both sides of the marketplace (if applicable). Skip if not a marketplace/platform.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 4 (Market Gaps) complete

## Actions

### 5.1 Analyze Demand Side

```
Demand side (customers/users):
- Who are the target customers?
- What are their current pain points?
- How do they solve this problem today?
- What would make them switch to our solution?
- How many potential customers exist?
```

### 5.2 Analyze Supply Side (if applicable)

```
Supply side (suppliers/partners):
- Who would provide the service/product?
- What are their current pain points?
- What value does our platform provide to them?
- How many potential suppliers exist?
- What would make them join our platform?
```

### 5.3 Document Assumptions and Risks

```
Assumptions to validate:
- [Assumption 1] — Status: [VALIDATED / NEEDS VALIDATION]
- [Assumption 2] — Status: [VALIDATED / NEEDS VALIDATION]

Risks:
- [Risk 1] — Mitigation: [Strategy]
- [Risk 2] — Mitigation: [Strategy]
```

Tag assumptions as `[VALIDATED]` or `[NEEDS VALIDATION]` based on evidence.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Demand side analyzed with customer pain points
- [ ] Supply side analyzed (if applicable)
- [ ] Assumptions documented and tagged
- [ ] Risks identified with mitigation strategies
