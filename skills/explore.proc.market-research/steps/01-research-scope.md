# Step 1: Define Research Scope and Questions

## Objective

Clarify what you need to learn and what decisions this research will inform.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Context baseline available (problem statement, domain)
- [ ] Product concept defined
- [ ] Slug confirmed

## Actions

### 1.1 Define Research Scope

```
Market Research Scope

I need to understand:

1. **Research objective**: [What decision will this research inform?]
   - e.g., "Validate that market size justifies MVP investment"

2. **Key research questions**:
   - Market size: [What is the total addressable market?]
   - Growth: [Is the market growing or declining?]
   - Competition: [Who are the key players and what do they offer?]
   - Market gap: [What unmet needs exist?]
   - Demand: [Do customers have this problem?]
   - Supply: [Are there enough suppliers/partners?]
   - Regulatory: [What compliance requirements exist?]

3. **Geographic scope**: [Global / Regional / Single country or metro area]

4. **Industry segment**: [Healthcare / Fintech / E-commerce / etc.]

5. **Timeline**: [When do you need findings?]

Please confirm this scope or provide corrections.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to confirm research scope.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Research objective defined
- [ ] Key questions identified
- [ ] Geographic scope and industry segment confirmed
- [ ] Human has confirmed research scope
