# Step 4: Document Copy and Disclosure Requirements

## Objective

Specify what must be shown to users and when, including required disclosures, consent mechanisms, mandatory notices, prohibited claims, and required warnings.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 3 (Accessibility) complete

## Actions

### 4.1 Required Disclosures

For each disclosure (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Notice, etc.):
- When shown
- Where shown
- Content requirements
- Language requirements
- Update frequency and notification

### 4.2 Consent Mechanisms

- Opt-in vs opt-out by data use type
- Granular consent requirements
- Consent withdrawal method and effect
- Consent record requirements

### 4.3 Mandatory Notices

- Cookie notices
- Data breach notifications (timeline, content, method)
- Service change notifications
- Price change notifications

### 4.4 Prohibited Claims

- Claims that cannot be made (e.g., "100% secure")
- Alternative acceptable wording

### 4.5 Required Warnings

- When and where warnings must appear
- Required warning content

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Required disclosures documented with timing and content
- [ ] Consent mechanisms specified per data use
- [ ] Mandatory notices listed with timelines
- [ ] Prohibited claims and required warnings documented
