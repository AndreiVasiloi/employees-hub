# Step 6: Create Compliance Risk Register

## Objective

Document what goes wrong if compliance requirements are missed, with likelihood, impact, consequences, mitigation, detection, and response.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 5 (Evidence) complete

## Actions

### 6.1 Identify Compliance Risks

For each compliance requirement, assess the risk of non-compliance:

**Per risk, document**:
- Requirement it relates to
- What goes wrong if missed
- Likelihood (High / Medium / Low)
- Impact (High / Medium / Low)
- Consequences: Legal, Financial, Reputational, Operational
- Mitigation strategies
- Detection methods
- Response plan
- Owner
- Status (Open / Mitigated / Accepted)

### 6.2 Prioritize Risks

Categorize all risks:
- **Critical** (must address before launch)
- **High** (must address in MVP)
- **Medium** (address in Phase 2)
- **Low** (monitor and address as needed)

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All compliance risks identified and documented
- [ ] Each risk has likelihood, impact, and consequences
- [ ] Mitigation and response plans defined
- [ ] Risks prioritized by severity
- [ ] Owners assigned
