# Step 4: Discover Technical Opportunities

## Objective

Identify what technology enables — automation, AI, personalization, analytics, real-time capabilities, and progressive enhancement.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 3 (Technical Constraints) complete

## Actions

### 4.1 Automation Opportunities

For each opportunity:
- What can be automated, benefit
- Feasibility, effort, impact, priority

### 4.2 AI and ML Opportunities

For each opportunity:
- What AI can enable, use case, technology type
- Feasibility, data requirements, training needs
- Effort, impact, risks (bias, accuracy), priority

### 4.3 Personalization Opportunities

For each opportunity:
- What can be personalized, benefit
- Data source, feasibility, privacy considerations
- Effort, impact, priority

### 4.4 Instrumentation and Analytics

For each opportunity:
- What can be tracked, insight gained
- Events to track, tools, privacy compliance
- Effort, impact, priority

### 4.5 Real-Time Capabilities

For each opportunity:
- What can be real-time, benefit
- Technology (WebSockets, SSE, polling)
- Feasibility, scalability, effort, impact, priority

### 4.6 Progressive Enhancement

For each opportunity:
- What can be enhanced for capable devices
- Baseline vs enhancement, detection, fallback
- Effort, impact, priority

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Opportunities identified across all categories
- [ ] Each scored for feasibility, effort, and impact
- [ ] High-impact opportunities highlighted
