# Step 2: Domain Interview — Constraints and Regulations

```
Domain Interview — Part 2: Constraints & Regulations

Thank you. Now I need to understand what constrains architectural decisions in this domain:

1. Are there regulatory requirements that apply?
   (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOX, FDA, industry-specific regulations)
   For each: what data or processes does it govern? What are the key compliance requirements?

2. Are there technical constraints?
   (e.g., legacy systems that must be integrated, technology mandates, performance baselines)

3. Are there organizational constraints?
   (e.g., team structure, deployment cadence, approval processes, vendor relationships)

4. Are there timeline or budget constraints that typically affect architecture?

If no regulations apply, that's fine — not every domain is regulated.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to provide constraints and regulatory landscape.
