# Step 3: Domain Interview — Patterns and Authority

```
Domain Interview — Part 3: Patterns & Authority

Now I need to understand common architectural patterns and authority structures:

1. What architectural patterns are commonly used in this domain?
   For each: when is it appropriate? When does it fail?
   (e.g., event sourcing, CQRS, saga, choreography, data mesh, etc.)

2. What are the typical "sources of truth" in this domain?
   (e.g., in healthcare: clinical record system, consent management, formulary database)
   Who or what is authoritative for different kinds of decisions?

3. What quality attributes matter most in this domain?
   Rank the top 5: latency, throughput, availability, security, compliance,
   auditability, cost, maintainability, scalability, observability

4. Are there domain-specific hardening checks that should always be run?
   (e.g., in healthcare: HIPAA data flow validation; in payments: reconciliation completeness)
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to provide patterns, authority structures, and quality priorities.
