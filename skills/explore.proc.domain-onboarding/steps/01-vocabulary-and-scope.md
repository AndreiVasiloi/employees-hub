# Step 1: Domain Interview — Vocabulary and Scope

Conduct a structured interview to capture the domain's core vocabulary and scope.

```
Domain Interview — Part 1: Vocabulary & Scope

Domain: [Name provided by human]
Industry vertical: [Identified or asked]

I need to understand this domain well enough to support HLD work. Let me ask some questions:

1. What are the 10-20 most important terms in this domain?
   (I need definitions, not just names — what does each term mean in YOUR context?)

2. Are there terms that mean different things in different contexts?
   (e.g., "account" in banking vs identity management)

3. What is the core business process or workflow this domain supports?

4. What are the main bounded contexts or sub-domains?

Please share what you can — I'll organize it into a structured vocabulary.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to provide domain vocabulary and scope answers.
