# Method: How Might We (HMW) + Starbursting

**Primitives used:** `reframe` (primary), `associate`
**Best for:** Problem definition, early-stage exploration, Design Thinking workshops
**Cognitive mode:** Flexibility — generating many framings before committing

## HMW Protocol

1. Take the problem statement and generate 5-8 HMW questions at varying abstraction:
   - 2 narrow (specific pain points)
   - 2 broad (underlying needs or desires)
   - 2 inverted (how might we make this worse?)
   - 1-2 analogical (how might we apply [domain X]'s approach?)
2. For each HMW, generate 3-5 quick ideas that answer the question
3. Identify which HMW framings produced the most interesting ideas — those are the framings worth pursuing

## Starbursting Extension

Instead of answers, generate questions:
- Who is affected and who isn't?
- What happens before/after the problem?
- Where does this manifest and where doesn't it?
- When is this worst? When is it irrelevant?
- Why do current solutions fail?
- How do adjacent domains handle this?

Use the questions as stimuli for idea generation — each non-obvious question is a potential HMW reframing.
