# Method: Round-Robin Perspectives

**Primitives used:** Mode switching, `reframe`, `evaluate`
**Best for:** Stakeholder analysis, ensuring diverse viewpoints, checking blind spots
**Cognitive mode:** Sequential perspective taking

## Protocol

1. Identify 4-6 relevant perspectives/stakeholders for the problem
   - Always include: end user, business owner, technical implementer
   - Add domain-specific: regulator, competitor, skeptic, enthusiast, newcomer

2. For each perspective, generate:
   - What would this person want from a solution?
   - What would they fear or resist?
   - What would they suggest that others wouldn't?

3. Synthesize: Where do perspectives align? Where do they conflict? Conflicts are where the interesting design decisions live.

## Expected Output

Perspective matrix + alignment/conflict analysis + insights for ideation.
