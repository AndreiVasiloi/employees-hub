# Method: SCAMPER

**Primitives used:** `transform` (primary), `evaluate`
**Best for:** Incremental improvement, product iteration, feature variation
**Cognitive mode:** Persistence — deep exploration of one concept through operators

## Protocol

1. State the base concept/product/feature clearly
2. Walk through each SCAMPER operator in order:
   - **Substitute:** What component, material, person, or process could be swapped?
   - **Combine:** What could be merged? Two features into one? Two audiences?
   - **Adapt:** What could be borrowed from another domain, product, or era?
   - **Modify:** What if you changed size, shape, speed, frequency, intensity?
   - **Put to other use:** Who else could use this? What other problem could it solve?
   - **Eliminate:** What could be removed and still deliver value?
   - **Reverse/Rearrange:** What if the sequence, hierarchy, or layout changed?
3. For each operator, generate 2-3 concrete variants (not abstract musings)
4. After all 7 operators: cluster the best variants and evaluate

## Expected Output

14-21 variants organized by operator, then clustered by quality.
