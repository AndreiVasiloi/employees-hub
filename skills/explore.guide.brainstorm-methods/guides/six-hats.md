# Method: Six Thinking Hats (solo variant)

**Primitives used:** Mode switching (primary), `evaluate`, `associate`
**Best for:** Examining an idea from all angles, when user is stuck in one perspective, when evaluation is muddled
**Cognitive mode:** Sequential perspective shifts

## Protocol — apply each hat to the idea/problem

| Hat | Focus | Prompt |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Blue | Process | "What's our goal? What's the plan? What have we covered?" |
| White | Facts | "What do we know? What data exists? What's missing?" |
| Red | Intuition | "What's the gut reaction? What feelings does this trigger?" |
| Yellow | Benefits | "What's the best case? What works? What's the upside?" |
| Black | Risks | "What could go wrong? What are the weaknesses?" |
| Green | Creativity | "What alternatives exist? What's unconventional? What if...?" |

## Solo Adaptation

- Don't just list answers under each hat — actually shift reasoning mode
- Under Yellow: be genuinely optimistic, find the strongest version
- Under Black: be genuinely critical, find the real risks (not strawmen)
- Under Green: generate 3-5 new alternatives or modifications
- Order: Blue (set up) → White (facts) → Red → Yellow → Black → Green → Blue (synthesize)

## Expected Output

Section per hat with findings, ending with Blue hat synthesis and next steps.
