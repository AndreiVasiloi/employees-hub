# Explore

The Explore phase turns qualified Signals into shared understanding, product
requirements, solution options, and an actionable backlog.

## Folders

- `epics/` — large product or learning outcomes grouping related work.
- `decisions/` — important product, architecture, and process decisions with rationale.
- `prds/` — product requirements, workflows, acceptance criteria, and scope boundaries.
- `domain/` — domain concepts and business rules.
- `architecture/` — system structure, boundaries, data flow, and deployment topology.
- `design/` — UX/UI journeys, interaction patterns, wireframes, and accessibility considerations.
- `sources/` — external references and evidence used during exploration.

High-level designs (`hlds/`) are deferred until a subsystem requires that level
of technical detail.
